# Staging Is QA's Job

Everything ships through QA, which validates it in staging. Do not try to
prove, verify, or ask for proof of behavior in staging, and never let it block
work, a finding, a review verdict, or a "done" call.

When an issue, ticket, comment, or checklist calls for staging verification,
leave it to QA. Skip it rather than raising it as a gap or a question. The one
exception is when I explicitly ask you to check something in staging.
