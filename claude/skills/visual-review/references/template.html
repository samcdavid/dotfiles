Product risks

mediumP1 Duplicates lose tags and attachments

A researcher who duplicates a question gets a copy without its tags or attachments, while Copy to study keeps them. Two actions that look alike behave differently.

unsupportedNo doc or ticket says whether a duplicate keeps tags and attachments; docs/guides/questions.md:40 only describes Copy to study.
